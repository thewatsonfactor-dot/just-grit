Why it’s different

It never guesses.

  • ✓The proof and the pitch come from the same sentence. If a review says the wait was long, the email is about the wait — not about something else the software would rather sell.
  • ✓No AI writing your emails. Every line is built from what was actually found, so it can’t invent a complaint or a compliment you can’t back up.
  • ✓A reply is treated like the emergency it is. It goes to the top of today’s list. The automated follow-ups stop the moment a human answers.
  • ✓Your data is yours. Every business runs in its own separate database. Nobody else’s leads can end up in your queue — not by policy, by construction.
  • ✓Calls are made by a person, one at a time. Nothing dials a list on its own. Texts only ever answer a call that came in first.
  • ✓A review card is drawn, never generated. A model re-typing a customer’s words is how a typo ends up in a quote with their name on it. Their words go on the card exactly as they wrote them.
