Facebook & Instagram

        
Connect your Facebook Page once and Instagram comes with it (if your
          Instagram is a Business account linked to that Page). Then the Social tab can post,
          schedule, and make pictures.

        

        

          
Connect

          
Open developers.facebook.com/tools/explorer, pick your app, press
            Generate Access Token with the boxes pages_show_list,
            pages_manage_posts, instagram_basic and
            instagram_content_publish ticked, and paste the token here. It's used once to
            fetch your Page's own key, then thrown away.

          

            

          
Make it permanent (recommended): your app's ID and secret
            
The token from Graph API Explorer lasts about an hour, and so does a Page key made from it.
              With your app's ID and secret (developers.facebook.com → your app → App settings → Basic) the app
              stretches it first, and the Page key it keeps doesn't expire. Saved here, never shown again.
