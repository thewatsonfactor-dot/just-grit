Your details

Used at the bottom of emails. The mailing address is required by law on any business email you send.

Calling (Telnyx)

Click a lead's Call via Telnyx button on the Today queue and it dials your own phone first - only once you pick up does it dial the lead and connect you both. Nothing here ever dials the lead on its own; that's what keeps this inside the manual-dialing rule for calling cell phones.

Mailbox (reply & bounce detection)

Without this, every email you send stays unmeasured - a dead address and a prospect who wasn't interested look identical, so there's no way to tell a list problem from a copy problem. This reads the mailbox read-only (it never sends, deletes, moves or marks anything read) and gives each sent email a real outcome. Bounces, out-of-offices and autoresponders are filed automatically; a real human answer is left for you to judge with one tap, because a scoreboard fed by a guessing machine is no better than an empty one.

Stored in this app's own settings on your Mac. Namecheap PrivateEmail uses your normal mailbox password.

What this business sells

Every cold email is built from three sentences per offer, and the engine will not write one until they exist — that's deliberate, so a new business can never go out borrowing somebody else's pitch. Write them the way you'd say them out loud to an owner.

Start from a few questions instead of a blank form

The system can read a website and see what a business sells. It can never see what's broken at a customer the moment before they buy — and that's the part that decides who gets written to. Only you know it, so it's the one thing worth typing out.

Snapshots

A snapshot is this business's configuration in a file — the offers, the call windows, how many days between follow-ups. Load it into the next customer and their setup is a tune, not a build.
It carries none of the things that make this workspace this workspace: no sender name or address, no phone numbers, no API keys, no hostname, no sending cap, and not one lead, contact or email. That isn't a filter — those are never read.

Naming the vertical is what pulls its calling hours into the file.
